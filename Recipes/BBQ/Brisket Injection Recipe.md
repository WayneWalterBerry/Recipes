# Brisket Injection Recipe

## Ingredients

- 2 Cups Beef Broth
- 2 Tablespoons Worestershire saure
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper.

## Directions

1. Whisk all ingredients together until fully combined.
2. Inject into the brisket using a meat injector before smoking.
