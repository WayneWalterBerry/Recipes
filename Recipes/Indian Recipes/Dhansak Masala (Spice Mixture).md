# Dhansak Masala (Spice Mixture)

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 teaspons [Garam Masala](https://sites.google.com/site/leplatprincipal/recipes/indian-recipes/garam-masala-spice-mixture)
- 1/2 teaspoon Star Anise Powder
- 1/4 teaspoon Nutmeg

## Directions

Combine and grind in a spice grinder.

Source: [Le Plat Principal](https://sites.google.com/site/leplatprincipal/recipes/indian-recipes/dhansak-masala-spice-mixture)
