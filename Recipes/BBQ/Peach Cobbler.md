# Peach Cobbler

## Ingredients

### Filling

- 10 ripe peaches, peeled, sliced
- 3 tablespoons almond liqueur
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 1 table spoon molasses
- 3 tablespoons potatoe starch

### Biscuit Dough

- 2 cups flour
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar, plus 1 1/2 tablespoons for the top
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 stick butter, cubed and chilled
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- 1 egg white, for washing

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the peaches, liqueur, nutmeg, brown sugar, granulated sugar, molasses and potatoe starch. Mix together. Pour mixture into a 13-inch-by-9-inch casserole dish.
3. Combine the flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder in a large bowl and mix. Cut in the butter with a fork until the dough resembles sand. Slowly add the buttermilk until the dough comes together. You may not need all of the buttermilk.
4. Place the dough on a floured surface and knead it into a 13-inch-by-9-inch rectangle about 1/2-inch thick and place over the peach filling. Use egg white as a wash, brushing on top of dough. Sprinkle the 1 1/2-tablespoons of sugar on top. Cook the cobbler until the top is golden brown, about 40 minutes.
