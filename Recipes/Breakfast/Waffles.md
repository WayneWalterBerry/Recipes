# Waffles

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 Cup Wheat Flour
- 1 1/2 Cup All Purpose Flour
- 1/2 Cup Brown Sugar
- 6 Teaspoons Baking Power
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 Large Eggs
- 2 1/2 Cup Milk
- 3/4 Cup Vegetable Oil
- 1/4 Cup Honey

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl combine all dry ingredients with a whisk.
2. In a large bowl whisk together milk and eggs until frothy.
3. Into the milk/eggs slowing pour oil and whisk at the same time to create a suspension.
4. Add dry ingredients to the suspension and combine until clumps are gone, don't over mix.
5. Gently Stir In Honey.
6. Wait 5 Minutes for baking powder to activate.
