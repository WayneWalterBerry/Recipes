# Hummas

Servings: 6

## Ingredients

- 2 1⁄2 cups water
- 3⁄4 cup chickpea flour (Garbanzo)
- 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1⁄4 teaspoons cumin, ground
- 1⁄4 teaspoons salt
- 1 lemon, juice of
- 1⁄4 cup tahini
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

## Directions

1. Pour 1/2 cup of water in 1 2 quart sauce pan.
2. Add all the ingredients except the garbanzo bean flour.
3. Whisk in the garbanzo bean flour to make a paste.
4. Whisk in slowly the remaining water.
5. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally.
6. Continue stirring and simmer for 3 minutes.
7. Remove from heat, cover and cool.
8. Use as a sandwich spread or as a dip.

Note: quantities of lemon juice, salt and garlic can be adjusted to preference.
