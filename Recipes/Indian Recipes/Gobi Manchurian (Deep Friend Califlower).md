# Gobi Manchurian (Deep Friend Califlower)

![Gobi Manchurian (Deep Friend Califlower)](<./Gobi Manchurian (Deep Friend Califlower).jpg>)

## Ingredients

### Deep Fried Cauliflower

- 1 medium sized cauliflower, cut into florets, placed in salted hot water for 10-12 mts, drained
- 2 tablespoons maida/all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons cornflour
- 1/2 tablespoon rice flour (optional)
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic-green chilli paste
- approx a little less than 1/2 cup warm water
- salt to taste

### For sauce

- 1/4 cup spring onions, finely chopped,
- 1/2 red bell pepper, finely sliced
- 1 1/2 tablespoons finely minced garlic
- 1/2 tablespoon finely sliced ginger
- 2 finely chopped green chillis
- 1 teaspoon red chilli pwd (preferably Kashmiri)
- 2 teaspoons soya sauce
- 1 tablespoon chilli sauce
- 2 teaspoons vinegar
- 3 tablespoons tomato sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon brown sugar (optional)
- salt as required
- 1 1/2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped coriander leaves

## Directions

### Deep Fried Cauliflower

1. In a large pot bring water to a boil and add about 2 tablespoons of salt.
2. Heat oil for deep frying in a heavy bottomed vessel.
3. In a bowl, combine maida, cornflour, rice flour, salt, ginger-garlic-green chilli paste and warm water to form a batter.
4. Dip the washed and drained florets into the batter such that its well coated.
5. Place each floret that is well coated with the batter into the hot oil. Do not crowd the vessel. Reduce flame and deep fry till the gobi is almost cooked. Increase the flame towards the end of the cooking process and fry the gobi till it turns golden brown.
6. Remove onto absorbent paper and keep aside.

### Sauce

1. Heat oil in a large wok and once the oil is piping hot,
2. Add the chopped garlic and stir fry for a few seconds. Add the green chillis and ginger and stir fry on high for a few seconds.
3. Add the whites of spring onions and stir fry on high for 3-4 minutes, constantly tossing them.
4. Add the sliced bell pepper and stir fry for another 3 minutes. They should retain their crunch.
5. Reduce to medium heat and add the brown sugar, soya sauce, tomato ketchup, chilli sauce and vinegar. Combine well and cook for 2 mts.
6. Turn to low and reduce until sauce becomes a syrup.

### Combine

1. Plate the deep fried cauliflower, drizzy sauce on top of cauliflower.
