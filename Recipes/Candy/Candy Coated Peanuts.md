# Candy Coated Peanuts

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 pound raw spanish peanuts
- Johnny's Seasoning Salt

## Directions

1. Cook in thick bottom pot on medium high, stirring constantly until coated & all water is gone.
2. Spread on greased cookie sheet. Salt with popcorn salt or Johnny's Seasoning Salt.
3. Bake at 275 for 15 minutes. Stir and repeat for another 15 minutes.
