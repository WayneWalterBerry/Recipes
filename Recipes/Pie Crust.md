# Pie Crust

## Ingredients

- 15 Tablespoons Shortening
- 2.5 Cups Flour
- 1 Teaspoon Salt
- 9-12 Tablespoons of Water

## Directions

1. Cut shortening into floor and salt using a pastry knife, until particles are the size of coursed crumbs. Sprinkle with cold water, 1 tablespoon at a time, tossing with fork until all flour is moistened and pastry almost cleans side of bowl.
2. Gather pastry into a ball. Divided in half. Shape into flattened round on a lightly floured silicon mat.

Source: [Le Plat Principal](https://sites.google.com/site/leplatprincipal/recipes/pie-crust)
