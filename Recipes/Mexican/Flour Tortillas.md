# Flour Tortillas

Makes about a dozen tortillas

## Ingredients

- 3 Cups (24 oz) Unbleached Flour
- ½ Tablespoon Salt
- 3 oz Manteca or Vegetable Shortening
- 1 cup (8 oz) Room Temperature water.
- 1/2 cup (4 oz) Extra Flour for sprinkling

## Directions

Making homemade tortillas is fun and there's very little kitchen equipment needed. After a few times, you'll have these steps down to memory.

1. Mix Flour and Salt together in a bowl (except for the extra flour).
2. Mix in the Manteca or Vegetable Shortening By Hand.
3. Mix in the water a little bit at a time until the dough is soft and sticky.
4. Kneed the dough for a bit and make about 18 small dough balls.
5. Heat up the griddle or frying pan. Make it medium to high temperature.
6. Sprinkle some flour (about one teaspoon) on a cutting board.
7. Break out the rolling pin and get a dough ball.
8. Put the dough ball on top of the sprinkled flour.
9. Put the rolling pin in the top center of the ball and start to roll out a little at a time.
10. Make sure to turn the flattened ball out now and then and sprinkle flour when it starts to stick.
11. Roll it out into a circle and make it fairly thin in thickness.
12. Lay your tortilla on the hot griddle or frying pan. It takes just about 45 – 60 seconds per side to cook.
13. Flip it when it starts to have a few nice brown speckles.
14. Cover tortillas with a towel. You'll do this with all the tortillas to keep them warm.
15. Enjoy your homemade tortillas with some butter, fresh salsa or stuff them with your favorite ingredients!
