# Beetroot Poriyal (Beet Curry)

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons oil
- 2 teaspoons black mustard seeds
- 2 tablespoons split black lentils
- 1 red onion finely chopped
- 4-5 curry leaves
- 2 teaspoons green chili paste
- 2 cups beetroot: (peeled, washed and grated)
- 1/4 cup shredded coconut
- Salt to taste.

## Preparation

1. Remove beet tops and peel the beetroot. Wash. Cube the beet root in 1/4 inch cubes.
2. Heat oil in a pan add mustard seeds and split black lentils after it sputters.
3. Add onion. Fry till it becomes translucent.
4. Add green chilies and curry leaves. Fry them all together for 3-4 minutes.
5. Add pureed beet root.
6. Cook till beetroot becomes soft and fully cooked about 1 hour.
7. Salt to taste.
8. Turn off heat add coconut and mix it all together.
9. Garnish it with coriander leaves.

Serve hot with chapathi or rice.
