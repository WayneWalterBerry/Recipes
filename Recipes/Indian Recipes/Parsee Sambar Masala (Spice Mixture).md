# Parsee Sambar Masala (Spice Mixture)

## Ingredients

- 3/4 teaspoon fenugreek powder
- 1/2 teaspoon black mustard powder
- 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground pepper

## Directions

Combine and grind in a spice grinder.
