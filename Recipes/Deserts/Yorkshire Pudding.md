# Yorkshire Pudding

## Ingredients

- 4 Large Eggs
- 2 cups Whole Milk
- 280 grams All Purpose Flour (~ 2 Cups)
- 2 Tablespoons of drippings for batter (plus 2 more tablespoons for coating the pan)

## Directions

1. Blend All Ingredients In Blender.
2. Get Cast iron skillet hot In 400 degree oven.
3. Add 2 Tablespoons of drippings to Pan, coat pan and sides.
4. Pour Batter in Center.
5. Cook 30 minutes at 400 degrees.
