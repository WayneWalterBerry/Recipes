# Casa Que Pasa Potato Burrito Sauce

At Casa this is made with the house red sauce, which is made with chiles de arbol, onions and tomatoes. It's a common taqueria red sauce, but there's really not many common commercial equivalents. This recipe substitutes Tapatio. There are so many flavors here that the difference shouldn't be apparent.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 Tablespoon chile de arbol sauce, e.g. Tapatio
- 1.5 teaspoons Worcestershire
- 3/4 teaspoon New Mexico red chile powder, mild
- 3/4 teaspoon garlic, crushed
- 3/4 teaspoon yellow prepared mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Directions

1. Whisk all ingredients together until fully combined. Refrigerate until ready to use.
