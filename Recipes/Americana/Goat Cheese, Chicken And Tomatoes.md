# Goat Cheese, Chicken And Tomatoes

## Ingredients

- 3 ½ pounds de boned chicken thighs with the skin on.
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 slices of thick bacon
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 (28-ounce) can whole plum tomatoes
- 1 large basil sprig, plus more chopped basil for serving
- 8 ounces goat cheese

## Preparation

1. Heat oven to 400 degrees. Pat chicken dry and season with salt and pepper.
2. In a large oven-proof skillet, warm oil over medium-high heat. Add bacon and cook, stirring frequently, until browned. Use a slotted spoon to transfer bacon to a paper-towel-lined plate.
3. Add chicken to skillet skin side down. Sear in batches with the skin-side down until well browned on all sides, about 5 minutes. Transfer to a large plate. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon oil.
4. Add garlic and red pepper flakes to skillet; cook on low for 1 minute, don't burn the garlic.
5. Add whole tomatoes gently squash them with a potato masher. Cook until sauce thickens somewhat, about 10 minutes.
6. Return chicken to skillet. Slice medallions of goat cheese and add between chicken. Transfer skillet to oven and cook, uncovered, until chicken is no longer pink, about 30 minutes.
7. Garnish chopped basil before serving.
